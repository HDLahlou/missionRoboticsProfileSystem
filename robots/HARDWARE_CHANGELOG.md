# Hardware and safety changelog

Every change to a `hardware.yaml` or `safety.yaml` file must add an entry here.
(The pre-commit hook in `scripts/hooks/` enforces this once you run
`./scripts/install_hooks.sh`.)

Format: date | robot | who | what changed | why | reviewed by

| Date | Robot | Who | What changed | Why | Reviewed by |
|------|-------|-----|--------------|-----|-------------|
| YYYY-MM-DD | (all) | (bootstrap) | Initial files created with FILL_ME_IN placeholders | Repo set-up | (pending) |
